Web 2.0

Web 2.0 refers to the second generation of the World Wide Web, characterized by increased user interactivity, collaboration, and the rise of social media platforms.

Key Features of Web 2.0:

  • Dynamic web pages with enhanced interactivity
  • User-generated content and social media platforms
  • Collaboration and sharing of information among users
