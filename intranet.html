Intranet

An intranet is a private network that is accessible only to an organization's members, employees, or authorized users. It is used to share information, resources, and services within the organization.

Key Features of Intranets:

  • Restricted access to authorized users within the organization
  • Facilitates internal communication and collaboration
  • Provides a centralized platform for sharing information and resources
