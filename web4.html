Web 4.0

Web 4.0 refers to the fourth generation of the World Wide Web, characterized by the integration of physical and digital worlds, the Internet of Things (IoT), and advanced artificial intelligence.

Key Features of Web 4.0:

  • Integration of physical and digital worlds through IoT devices
  • Advanced artificial intelligence for personalized user experiences
  • Enhanced connectivity and real-time data processing
